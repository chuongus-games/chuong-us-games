🔍 Focus Lock

Watch the yellow dot, then track it as every dot mixes together. When they stop, tap the one you tracked. Wrong tap costs a life, you have 3.

One dot flashes, then blends into a swarm of identical ones that drift and bounce for several seconds. Focus Lock is a free attention game built on real cognitive science, multiple object tracking, and losing your dot for even a second ends the streak.
