💠 Neon Slice

Swipe / drag across the orbs to slice them. Slicing a bomb ends the run instantly.

Neon Slice is a free fast-paced slicing game you can play online in your browser. Orbs launch from the bottom and arc through the air — swipe through them to score, but one bomb sliced by mistake ends the run, which makes staying accurate at speed one of the hardest slicing challenges you'll find online.
