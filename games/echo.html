💫 Echo Tap

Tap / click / press Space the instant the shrinking ring lines up with the dashed target ring. Too early, too late, or no tap at all ends the run.

Echo Tap is a free online rhythm precision game: a ring shrinks toward a fixed target, and you must tap the exact instant they align. Every successful tap speeds things up and shrinks the tolerance window, making sustained precision one of the hardest timing challenges you'll find in a browser.
