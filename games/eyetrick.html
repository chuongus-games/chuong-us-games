👁️ Eye Trick

A color word appears painted in a different ink color. Tap the button matching the INK COLOR, not what the word says, the time window shrinks as your score climbs. Wrong tap or timeout costs a life. 3 lives total.

Eye Trick runs on the Stroop effect, the classic trick where a color word printed in the wrong ink color messes with your brain. Tap the actual ink color, not the word, while the clock keeps shrinking. Free, and harder than it has any right to be.
