💡 Flash Grid

Tap the glowing BLUE cell before it fades out. Never tap the RED trap cell, that's instant game over. Missing a blue cell costs a life; you have 3.

A blue cell flashes, you tap it before it fades. A red one shows up sometimes, and touching that ends the game instantly. Flash Grid gets faster and meaner as your score climbs. Free, quick to learn, hard to keep clean.
